Where to Find Asian Groceries in Los Angeles

About this map. This 3D map shows the major Asian supermarket chains in Los Angeles County, colored by cuisine: Chinese/Taiwanese, Korean, Japanese, Filipino, Southeast Asian, and South Asian. The stores are not stored in this page. Every time the page opens, it asks the USDA SNAP Retailer Locations feature service for the stores that match a SQL filter, so new or closed stores show up automatically. LA Metro rail stations (from LA GeoHub) are added in gray so you can see which stores you can reach by train. Click any pin for details. Compared with the starting Esri sample, I replaced the Swiss mountain peaks with live LA data, changed the basemap, camera, symbols, labels, and popups, and added a legend.
